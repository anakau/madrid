Keeping Madrid livable and investable under extreme heat

Heat is no longer a seasonal nuisance — it is a systemic and sovereign risk to the value of Madrid. No single project, agency or balance-sheet can act alone. The Coalition orchestrates coordinated interventions across industry, policy and finance.

Explore the strategy See the demonstrators

The Challenge

Madrid is heading toward a ~47°C future

Rising temperatures put the city’s liveability, economy and future at risk. Extreme heat cascades through the city — from stressed infrastructure to whole-sector failures to social and economic disruption. It affects everything at once.

47°C Projected summer high by 2100 (RCP-8.5 scenario)
+8.5°C Added to night-time temperatures by the urban heat island
15→85 Tropical nights (>20 °C) per year, projected to 2100
3.5 M+ Residents to keep safe — and the city investable

OUR GOAL

Keep Madrid livable and investable.

1

Make the city’s heat liability visible — a “cooling balance sheet” of exceedances, vulnerable populations and infrastructure stress.

2

Authorize cooling commitments and mobilize resources through outcome contracts, procurement and risk-sharing facilities.

3

Align public, private, civic and financial actors so climate resilience becomes a shared dividend that compounds over time.

OUR APPROACH

An Outcome Accelerator for systemic change

A structured, multi-year, multi-actor programme that starts from whole-city outcomes — then orchestrates the whole system with flexible finance and shared accountability. Six capabilities work together as one platform.

Outcomes focus

Start from defined, whole-city outcomes — not individual projects — and accelerate toward collective intelligence.

Coalition of actors

Public, private, civic and financial actors implementing and experimenting together.

Portfolio of options

Scalable interventions combining technology, nature and design, contributing to shared outcomes.

Finance model

Outcome-enabling mechanisms: cooling bonds, outcome contracts, procurement and risk-sharing.

Learning system

Feedback loops and shared sensing that make the city’s heat liability visible and legible.

Institutional support

Governance and coordination designed to outlast political cycles.

The portfolio tool

A portfolio tool for composing interventions

Rather than one-off projects, the Coalition works from a living portfolio: a stack of interventions — from the physical fabric of the city down to the capital architecture that funds it. Actors compose a mix across the layers, and the tool makes the trade-offs, costs and shared outcomes legible.

Physical layer

Cool roofs Reflective surfaces Shaded corridors

Living layer

Biodiversity corridors Soil regeneration Evapotranspiration gardens

Intelligence layer

Environmental sensing Real-time thermal mapping Spatial AI

Care layer

Autonomous irrigation Drone-based inspection Aquifer recharge

Governance structures

Institutions Citizens Urban ecosystems

Capital architecture

Cooling bonds Outcome contracts Risk-sharing facilities
